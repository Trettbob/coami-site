CoAmi

Plan and build software with Ami, an AI assistant, one approved step at a time.

What CoAmi is

CoAmi is a place to plan and build a software project. You describe what you want in plain English. Ami, CoAmi’s AI assistant, asks questions until the plan is clear and writes it up as a Blueprint: what the project will do, and what it won’t.

Nothing is built until you approve the Blueprint. CoAmi then builds the project in steps and keeps the code in a repository for that project. You can open a preview, try it, and send feedback for the next step.

Ami is an AI and can make mistakes. You stay in charge: you approve the plan, and you decide what happens next. CoAmi is made by Trett Labs in the UK.

Private pilot

CoAmi is in a private pilot. From November 2026 we’re inviting one to three workspaces to try it.

It’s by invitation only. There’s no public sign-up, and the pilot is free.

Email we send

We only send email that’s needed to use CoAmi:

  • Invitations, only to people a workspace owner has invited.
  • Sign-in and account messages, such as a notice that your account details changed.
  • Updates about your own projects. You can switch these off in your settings.

We don’t send marketing email, and we never buy or rent email lists.

Our email comes from no-reply@coami.ai. Replies to that address aren’t read, so please use the contact address below.

If an email to you bounces, or you mark one of ours as spam, we stop emailing that address. We delete that record when your data is deleted.

If you got an email from us you didn’t expect, you can ignore it, or tell us and we’ll look into it.
