How it works

This is the journey we're building for the private pilot.

  1. 01

    Describe it

    Tell Ami what you want to build, in plain English.

  2. 02

    Agree the plan

    Ami asks questions to make the plan clear, and writes it up as a Blueprint: what the project will do, and what it won't.

  3. 03

    Approve, then build

    Nothing is built until you approve the Blueprint.

  4. 04

    Try it

    Open a preview, try it, and send feedback for the next step.

Ami

Ami is an AI.

Ami is an AI assistant, not a person. It can make mistakes, so you check and approve each step.

You stay in charge: you approve the plan, and you decide what happens next.
