What we're building into CoAmi

Here's what's planned. Some of it is being built for our private pilot.

CoAmi is in development. Features, timing and what's included may change. Nothing on this page is an offer or a promise that a feature will be available.

The core product

  • Ami

    An AI assistant that talks your idea through with you and helps turn it into a plan.

    Planned for the private pilot

  • Blueprint

    A clear written plan of what will be built, broken into small steps you can read and approve.

    Planned for the private pilot

  • Your own project space

    Each project gets its own private code repository, set up for you.

    Planned for the private pilot

  • Building

    CoAmi builds your project in small steps, one approved step at a time, and shows its progress as it goes.

    Planned for the private pilot

  • Previews and feedback

    See a preview of what's been built and tell Ami what to change.

    Planned for the private pilot

  • Progress emails

    An email when a preview is ready, when Ami needs your answer, or when a build is blocked.

    Planned for the private pilot

  • Needs You

    One place where decisions that need you wait for your approval.

    Planned

The Business Companion, designed to work together

  • Shared customer record

    One record for each of your customers that every feature uses, so details don't need typing twice.

    Planned

  • Ami across your business

    Ami shows what needs you today and drafts replies and follow-ups for you to approve.

    Planned

  • Email

    Read and reply to your email inside CoAmi, with Ami's suggested replies waiting for your OK.

    Planned

  • Calendar

  • Tasks

    Planned
